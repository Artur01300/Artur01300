## About me

I am a web developer based near Belley, in France. I obtained my Web Developer diploma in 2021 and I now run **SimpleWebs**, a web agency focused on practical, maintainable and human-centered digital solutions.

My work is not limited to making websites look good. A good website should be fast, readable, accessible, easy to maintain, useful for real users and understandable by search engines.

I help professionals with:

- custom website creation;
- SEO and local SEO optimization;
- e-commerce projects, especially Shopify;
- technical audits and website improvements;
- performance, accessibility and maintenance;
- clear support before, during and after launch.

I enjoy analyzing needs carefully, designing clean solutions, building with standards and improving projects over time.
